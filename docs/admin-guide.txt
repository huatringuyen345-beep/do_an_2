HUONG DAN KHU VUC QUAN LY
=========================

Dang nhap bang tai khoan nhan_vien hoac quan_tri tai:
  Website_Customer/pages/Login.php?area=staff

Database moi tao bang file SQL co tai khoan quan tri demo local. Neu tao
database bang migration, chay database/seeds/001_demo_admin.sql neu can
tao tai khoan demo. Doi mat khau sau lan dang nhap dau va khong su dung
tai khoan demo tren moi truong cong khai.

Tai khoan quan ly do nguoi co tham quyen cap; khong dang ky tu giao dien.
Khu vuc nghiep vu:
  Website_Manage/admin/modules/bookings/bookings.php   Don dat ve
  Website_Manage/admin/modules/trips/trips.php         Chuyen xe
  Website_Manage/admin/modules/fleet/fleet.php         Tuyen, xe va ghe
  Website_Manage/admin/modules/services/services.php   Don dich vu
  Website_Manage/admin/modules/support/contacts.php    Lien he
  Website_Manage/admin/modules/users/profile.php       Ho so tai khoan

URL cu trong Website_Manage/admin/ van chuyen den cac trang module moi.
Quan tri vien va nhan vien hien dung chung quyen nghiep vu; khong cap quyen
quan_tri cho tai khoan khach hang.

Tao mat khau bang password_hash(), khong luu mat khau goc. Sao luu database
truoc khi chinh sua truc tiep; khong import lai schema len database dang
van hanh. Khong dua thong tin ket noi that len Git.
