HUONG DAN KHU VUC KHACH HANG
============================

Trang chinh:
  Website_Customer/pages/index.php

Trang tai khoan:
  Website_Customer/pages/login.php       Dang nhap va tao tai khoan
  Website_Customer/pages/tai-khoan.php   Ho so, lich su ve va doi mat khau
  Website_Customer/pages/logout.php      Dang xuat qua POST va CSRF

Trang nghiep vu:
  dat-ve.php       Gui yeu cau dat ve
  lien-he.php      Gui tin nhan lien he
  tuyen-dung.php   Xem vi tri va gui thong tin ung tuyen

URL cu tai Website_Customer/frontend/ van duoc giu de tuong thich.
Ma trang nam trong pages/, giao dien dung chung trong includes/, CSS,
JavaScript va hinh anh trong assets/. Xu ly yeu cau nam trong backend/.

Khach hang dang nhap tai pages/login.php. Dat ve, gui lien he va nop ho so
yeu cau tai khoan. Du lieu gia, gio chay va mot so noi dung hien la mau;
can doi chieu voi thong tin thuc te truoc khi van hanh.
