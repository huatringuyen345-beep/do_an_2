HUONG DAN DUNG CHUNG CHO KHACH HANG VA NHAN VIEN
Nha xe Tai Nguyen
============================================================
1. MUC DICH

Tai lieu nay gom huong dan cho khach hang va nhan vien:
- Khach hang xem thong tin, tao tai khoan, dat ve va quan ly tai khoan.
- Nhan vien xu ly dat ve, chuyen xe, doi xe, don dich vu va lien he.

Huong dan rieng cho quyen quan tri (cap/tai khoan nhan vien va bao mat)
xem trong Website_Manage/HUONG-DAN-ADMIN.txt.

Khu vuc quan ly danh cho nhan vien va quan tri vien:
- Don dat ve va trang thai ve.
- Lich chay, chuyen xe va phan cong tai xe.
- Diem dung, tuyen duong, xe va ghe xe.
- Don dich vu, gui hang va trang thai van don.
- Yeu cau lien he va noi dung phan hoi khach hang.

Trang quan ly khong co chuc nang dang ky tai khoan nhan vien. Tai khoan
phai duoc quan tri vien cap truoc.


2. MO WEBSITE VA DANG NHAP

Buoc 1: Khoi dong Apache va MySQL/MariaDB (XAMPP hoac dich vu tuong ung).

Buoc 2: Mo trang chu khach hang:
http://localhost/Website_TaiNguyen/Website_Customer/frontend/

Khach hang dang nhap/tao tai khoan tai:
http://localhost/Website_TaiNguyen/Website_Customer/frontend/login.php

Nhan vien dang nhap tai:
http://localhost/Website_TaiNguyen/Website_Customer/frontend/login.php?area=staff
Tai khoan phai duoc quan tri vien cap truoc, dang hoat dong va co vai tro
quan_tri hoac nhan_vien. Nhan vien khong the tu dang ky tai khoan quan ly.

Nut hinh mat tro tren trang dang nhap co the hien/an mat khau. Nut hinh
trang/mo doi giao dien sang hoac toi.

2.1. HUONG DAN KHACH HANG

- Trang chu va cac trang gioi thieu, lich trinh, dich vu, tin tuc, ho tro
  co the xem ma khong can dang nhap.
- Tao tai khoan khach hang bang email va mat khau co it nhat 8 ky tu.
- Dat ve, gui lien he va nop ho so yeu cau dang nhap tai khoan khach hang.
- Trong "Tai khoan", khach hang co the cap nhat thong tin, xem ve va doi
  mat khau.
- Dang xuat sau khi dung xong, nhat la tren may tinh dung chung.

Huong dan chi tiet cho khach hang nam trong Website_Customer/HUONG-DAN.txt.


3. CAC MUC QUAN LY

3.1. DAT VE

Mo muc "Dat ve" de xem toi da 100 don moi nhat. Co the tim theo ma dat
ve, ten khach hang, so dien thoai va loc theo trang thai.

Moi dong hien thi ma don, thong tin khach, chuyen va ghe, thanh toan,
so tien, ngay dat va trang thai.

Cach cap nhat:
1. Chon trang thai moi o cot "Trang thai / cap nhat".
2. Bam "Luu".

Trang thai don gom:
- Cho xac nhan: don moi dang cho nhan vien xu ly.
- Da xac nhan: don da duoc nhan vien xac nhan.
- Da thanh toan: danh dau don da thanh toan.
- Da huy: don bi huy.
- Hoan tien: ghi nhan trang thai hoan tien.

Khi cap nhat, trang thai cua cac ve chi tiet cung duoc cap nhat: don da
xac nhan/da thanh toan thi ve duoc xac nhan; don da huy/hoan tien thi ve
chi tiet bi huy; cac trang thai con lai dua ve giu cho.

Luu y: cap nhat trang thai khong tu dong thu tien, tao giao dich, chuyen
tien hoan, gui email hay goi dien cho khach. Nhan vien can thuc hien va
xac nhan cac viec do theo quy trinh cua cong ty.


3.2. CHUYEN XE

Mo muc "Chuyen xe" de xem toi da 100 chuyen moi nhat, so ve da dat, xe,
tai xe, gia ve va trang thai.

Tao chuyen:
1. Chon tuyen dang hoat dong.
2. Chon xe dang hoat dong.
3. Chon tai xe dang hoat dong neu da phan cong; co the de trong.
4. Nhap gio khoi hanh, gio den du kien (khong bat buoc) va gia ve.
5. Chon trang thai, thuong la "Mo ban" khi bat dau nhan dat ve.
6. Bam "Tao chuyen xe".

Sua chuyen:
1. Bam "Sua" tai dong chuyen can sua.
2. Dieu chinh thong tin va bam "Luu chuyen xe".

Co the cap nhat rieng trang thai tai danh sach va bam "Luu".

Trang thai chuyen gom: Mo ban, Het cho, Dang chay, Hoan thanh, Da huy.
Chi chuyen co tuyen va xe dang hoat dong moi duoc chon khi tao/sua.

Truoc khi mo ban, kiem tra dung chieu tuyen, ngay gio, xe, tai xe, gia
ve va so ghe cua xe.


3.3. TUYEN & DOI XE

Trang nay gom bon phan: Diem dung, Tuyen duong, Xe va Ghe xe. Bam cac
lien ket dau trang de nhay den phan can quan ly.

Diem dung:
- Them ten diem, dia chi va tinh/thanh; so dien thoai la tuy chon.
- Bam "Them diem dung" de tao moi.
- Bam "Sua" tai dong tuong ung de chinh sua.

Tuyen duong:
- Dat ten tuyen, chon diem di va diem den khac nhau.
- Co the nhap khoang cach (km), thoi gian (phut), gia ve co ban.
- Chon Hoat dong hoac Tam ngung, sau do bam nut them/luu tuyen.
- Tuyen duoc chuyen xe su dung phai dang Hoat dong.

Xe:
- Nhap bien so, co the nhap ten xe, chon loai xe va trang thai.
- Trang thai xe gom Hoat dong, Bao tri, Ngung hoat dong.
- Xe dung cho chuyen moi phai dang Hoat dong.
- Cot "Ghe" hien so ghe da khai bao so voi suc chua cua loai xe.

Ghe xe:
- Chon xe, nhap ma ghe va tang, sau do bam "Them ghe".
- Ma ghe khong duoc trung trong cung mot xe.
- Co the sua ghe bang nut "Sua".
- Chi xoa ghe chua duoc gan vao ve. Neu ghe da co ve, database se tu choi
  xoa de tranh lam hong thong tin dat cho.

Nen tao diem dung truoc, sau do tao tuyen, xe va ghe, cuoi cung moi tao
chuyen xe.


3.4. DON DICH VU

Mo muc "Don dich vu" de tim don theo ma don, ten khach, so dien thoai
hoac loc theo trang thai. Man hinh hien dich vu, khach hang, lich su dung,
dia chi, tong tien va thong tin van don neu don la gui hang.

Cap nhat trang thai don:
1. Chon trang thai trong cot "Trang thai don".
2. Bam "Luu".

Trang thai don gom: Cho xu ly, Da xac nhan, Dang thuc hien, Hoan thanh,
Da huy.

Don co ma van don co them trang thai van chuyen:
Cho lay hang, Da nhan hang, Dang van chuyen, Da den noi, Da giao, That
bai, Da huy.

Cap nhat rieng trang thai don va trang thai van don cho dung tien trinh.
Neu don khong co chi tiet hang/ma van don thi khong the cap nhat trang
thai van don.


3.5. LIEN HE

Mo muc "Lien he" de xem noi dung khach gui. Co the loc theo trang thai:
Moi, Dang xu ly, Da phan hoi, Dong.

Cach xu ly:
1. Doc thong tin lien he va noi dung yeu cau.
2. Nhap noi dung tra loi vao o "Noi dung phan hoi".
3. Chon trang thai xu ly.
4. Bam "Luu xu ly".

Neu chon "Da phan hoi", bat buoc phai nhap noi dung tra loi. He thong luu
noi dung va gan nhan vien dang dang nhap la nguoi xu ly.

Luu y: man hinh nay chi luu phan hoi trong he thong; hien chua tu dong
gui email, tin nhan hay hien phan hoi cho khach.


4. DANG XUAT

Bam "Dang xuat" tren thanh ben (may tinh) hoac tren thanh tren (man hinh
nho). Sau khi dang xuat, trinh duyet tro ve trang dang nhap. Luon dang
xuat khi dung xong tren may tinh dung chung.


5. KHOI TAO DATABASE LAN DAU

Hai ung dung dung chung database website_tainguyen. Cau hinh ket noi nam
tai:
- Website_Customer/backend/database/config.php
- Website_Manage/config/config.php

Host, username va password phu thuoc cai dat tren may; kiem tra gia tri
trong hai file cau hinh neu khong ket noi duoc. Khong chia se thong tin
mat khau database.

Schema nam tai Website_Customer/backend/database/website_tainguyen.sql
va Website_Manage/database/website-tainguyen.sql.

Khong import file SQL len database dang co du lieu: cac file co the chua
lenh DROP TABLE va se xoa bang/du lieu cu truoc khi tao lai.

Sau khi khoi tao schema, neu cac danh muc hoac danh sach don trong, can
tao du lieu diem dung, tuyen, loai xe, xe, ghe, chuyen xe va cac du lieu
nghiep vu can thiet. Viec cap tai khoan nhan vien xem tai lieu admin rieng.
Khong thay doi ten cot/bang neu chua cap nhat dong bo code PHP.


6. XU LY SU CO THUONG GAP

Khong mo duoc trang / loi 404:
- Kiem tra Apache dang chay.
- Kiem tra thu muc Website_TaiNguyen nam trong document root.
- Dung dung dia chi o muc 2.

Bao loi khong ket noi duoc database:
- Kiem tra MySQL/MariaDB dang chay.
- Kiem tra host, database, username va password trong hai file cau hinh
  duoc liet ke o muc 5.
- Kiem tra database/schema da duoc tao.

Khong dang nhap duoc:
- Kiem tra email va mat khau, chu y khong nhap chuoi hash vao o mat khau.
- Tai khoan phai co vai tro quan_tri/nhan_vien va trang thai hoat_dong.
- Neu van loi, lien he quan tri vien kiem tra tai khoan va dat lai mat khau
  bang password_hash(). Khong gui mat khau cho nguoi khac.

Khong tao duoc chuyen:
- Kiem tra tuyen va xe dang Hoat dong.
- Kiem tra tai xe (neu chon) dang hoat dong va co vai tro tai_xe.
- Kiem tra ngay gio, gia ve va cac truong bat buoc.

Khong xoa/sua duoc du lieu:
- Du lieu co the dang duoc chuyen xe, ve hoac don khac tham chieu.
- Khong xoa bang/du lieu bang phpMyAdmin de vuot qua rang buoc; bao quan
  tri vien kiem tra lien ket truoc.

Neu trang bao "Phien lam viec khong hop le":
- Tai lai trang va gui lai bieu mau; khong dung nut Back de gui lai yeu
  cau cu sau khi da het phien.


7. LUU Y AN TOAN

- Chi chia se tai khoan cho dung nguoi duoc cap quyen.
- Khong de tai khoan dang nhap tren may tinh cong cong.
- Khong chia se anh chup/man hinh co mat khau, hash mat khau hay thong tin
  ket noi database.
- Khong import lai file website-tainguyen.sql vao database dang co du lieu.
- Khong tu y sua cau truc database khi chua sao luu va chua duoc quan tri
  vien dong y.
